# Not legal, tax, or investment advice

Planvest is a software research project. Nothing in this repository is:

- an offer of investment advice or a solicitation to buy or sell securities
- tax advice
- legal advice
- an invitation to share brokerage, HSA, or 401(k) login credentials

A product that recommends, reallocates, or trades HSA or 401(k) assets in the United States generally requires some combination of:

- SEC or state registered investment adviser status (Form ADV, Form CRS)
- broker-dealer registration or an introducing-broker agreement with a FINRA member
- an IRS-qualified HSA trustee or custodian
- for workplace 401(k): ERISA fiduciary status and plan-sponsor appointment

Counsel (securities, ERISA, and tax) must review any production launch. The gap IDs in `docs/API_GAPS.md` are an engineering map, not a legal opinion.
