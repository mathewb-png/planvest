    Match first

    The employer match is the only return that does not depend on the market. Contribute at least enough to collect it before you chase a hotter ticker.

    Funds, not stock picks

    A typical 401(k) menu is S&P 500, total market, small-cap, bonds, and a target-date fund. Those are the same markets as SPY, VTI, IWM, and BND below — not Apple or NVIDIA by themselves.

    History is not a promise

    Long-run US stock illustrations use about 10% a year because that is what a century of the S&P 500 did, including down years. Last year’s live return is shown so you can see the difference. One year is not a plan.
